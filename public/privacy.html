Calculations and browser preferences

Calculations run in your browser. Unpurify does not send your CP, HP, IVs, levels, filters, results, or typed search text to a server. Fonts, artwork, and game data load from this site's own files. Unpurify sets no cookies.

When you choose a language, the browser saves it in local storage under unpurify.language. When you set your statistics choice, it saves that choice under unpurify.analytics-consent.v1:https://unpurify.goatcounter.com/count. These preferences contain no user ID. They remain until you change them or clear this site's browser storage. They support the settings you request; the legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, with the interest of remembering those settings.

Optional usage statistics

Statistics are off by default. If you enable them, each valid advanced search sends the selected Pokémon's stable ID, form, and name. Each change between simple and advanced mode sends the selected mode. Page loads and typing send no statistics.

The purpose is to count calculator use and the most searched Pokémon. The legal basis is your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can use the calculator without consent.

Events go to https://unpurify.goatcounter.com/count. Requests omit cookies and referrer information. The server receives your IP address and browser headers. Only event totals by hour or day are stored. Sessions, individual pageviews, referrers, browser and system details, screen sizes, locations, and languages are disabled. The dashboard is private. Totals have no automatic deletion date.

GoatCounter is operated by Martin Tournoij in Ireland. Its servers are at Hetzner in Finland and Germany. According to its privacy policy, it does not store IP addresses or full User-Agent headers in its database. Deleted data can remain in backups for up to 30 days.

How to stop statistics

Return to the Usage statistics setting in the footer. Open it and clear Allow usage statistics. This stops future events and saves your choice. If you use another browser or device, change its choice too. Clearing this site's browser storage also returns statistics to the off state.

Withdrawal does not change the lawfulness of earlier processing. Previous totals cannot be assigned to your browser or removed as your individual history.
