# Unpurify

Compare your Shadow Pokémon before and after purification in Pokémon GO.
Check the IV change, calculate the new CP, and see the Stardust and Candy
cost before you purify.

**[Open the calculator](https://luisleineweber.github.io/unpurify/)**

## Use the calculator

### Enter IVs

Use this mode when you know your Pokémon's appraisal values.

1. Open your Shadow Pokémon in Pokémon GO and select **Appraise**.
2. Select **Enter IVs** in Unpurify.
3. Enter the Attack, Defense, and HP IVs. Each value must be from 0 to 15.
4. Compare the IVs, IV percentage, and appraisal before and after purification.

Each appraisal bar has three sections of 5 points. A full bar means 15 IV.
The HP IV is different from the hit points shown on your Pokémon's page.

For example, a Shadow Pokémon with **13 / 13 / 13** IVs becomes
**15 / 15 / 15**, or **100%**, after purification.

### Pokémon & CP

Use this mode to find possible IV combinations and calculate the new CP.

1. Select **Pokémon & CP**.
2. Select your Pokémon and its form, then enter its current CP.
3. Add maximum HP, level, or known IVs to reduce the number of results.
4. Select **Calculate possible IVs**.
5. Select a result that matches your appraisal to see the full comparison.

Use maximum HP, not the remaining HP after a battle. If the game shows
**32 / 49 HP**, enter **49**.

**CP alone cannot identify one IV combination.** Check the results against
the appraisal in Pokémon GO. This mode also shows the purification cost.

## Understand your result

- Purification adds 2 to each IV. Each IV remains capped at 15.
- Purification raises a Pokémon to level 25. A higher level stays the same.
- Purification removes the Shadow damage bonus. That bonus does not change
  the displayed CP.
- A higher IV percentage does not always mean better battle performance.
  Unpurify does not rate moves or rank Pokémon for raids or the GO Battle League.
- The calculator supports levels 1–50, including half levels. It does not
  include the Best Buddy level boost or Mega Pokémon.
- A Pokémon in the list may not be available as a Shadow Pokémon in the game.

## Languages

Unpurify supports English, German, Italian, Spanish, French, and Japanese,
including Pokémon names. Select a language in the header to save your choice
in the browser. Your inputs and results stay in place when you change languages.

On your first visit to the main page, Unpurify uses the first supported browser
language. It uses English if none of your browser languages are supported.

## Privacy

Calculations run in your browser. Your CP, HP, IVs, levels, filters, and typed
search text stay in your browser.

Optional usage statistics are off by default. You can turn them on or off
under **Usage statistics** in the footer. Read the
[privacy notice](https://luisleineweber.github.io/unpurify/privacy.html)
for details. The notice is in English.
